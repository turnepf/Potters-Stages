## Description

Potter's Stages keeps track of every piece you make, from the first spark of an idea to the final glaze firing.

Pottery takes patience. A bowl might sit leather hard for a day, dry for a week, then wait on two firings before it's done. Potter's Stages shows you at a glance where everything stands, so nothing gets forgotten on the shelf.

FIVE STAGES, ONE LOOK
• Idea: inspiration saved for later
• Leather Hard: time to trim, carve and attach handles
• Greenware: bone dry and waiting for the bisque firing
• Bisqueware: ready to glaze, or glazed and waiting on the glaze firing
• Finished: glaze-fired and done

PHOTOS AT EVERY STAGE
Take a photo or pick from your library and tag it with the stage. Each piece's cover shows it as it is right now, and its history shows how it got there.

SAVE INSPIRATION FROM ANYWHERE
Share a photo or link from Safari, Photos or almost any app straight into Potter's Stages to start a new idea or add to a piece you're already working on.

QUICK TO KEEP UP
Touch and hold a piece to move it to the next stage. Search titles and notes, merge two entries for the same pot, and flip between In Progress, Ideas and Done with one tap.

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
Your pieces sync through your own iCloud account to iPhone, iPad and Mac. Export a backup file whenever you like.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your pieces and photos stay in your own iCloud, and the app collects no data at all.

## Notes for App Review

No sign-in is needed. Pieces and photos are stored on the device and in the user's private iCloud (CloudKit); there is no server. To try it: tap + to add a piece, add a photo, then touch and hold the piece in the grid to advance it to the next stage. The ⋯ menu has Check iCloud Sync and Export/Import Backup. The share extension appears as "Potter's Stages" in the share sheet of Photos and Safari.
